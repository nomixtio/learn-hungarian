An open-source PWA built to follow Hungarian conversations with live English text, plus courses and practice — powered by Soniox, deployed on Cloudflare. Run your own instance; there is no hosted demo.

View on GitHub Self-host guide

You’ll need your own Soniox API key and a Cloudflare account. API usage is billed to you.

Courses

Structured vocabulary, ready to practice

Eight built-in courses cover the alphabet, numbers, greetings, and everyday topics. Each lesson includes Hungarian audio, English prompts, and speaking exercises.

Hungarian alphabet

All 44 letters with pronunciation examples — short and long vowels, familiar consonants, and digraphs like sz and gy.

Hungarian numbers

Count from zero through thousands. Work through basics, teens, tens, and compound numbers step by step.

Greetings & introductions

Informal hellos, polite time-of-day greetings, and phrases for meeting someone new.

Essential phrases

Politeness, communication when you do not understand, and handy travel vocabulary.

Colors

Basic and extended color words — from piros and kék to a wider palette.

Days of the week

The seven days (week starts on Monday in Hungary) plus related time words.

Months & seasons

All twelve months and the four seasons — with notes on Hungarian capitalization.

Food & drink

Everyday food vocabulary, meal names, and phrases for ordering at a restaurant.

Appearance

Light or dark — your choice

The app ships with a polished dark theme for low-light study sessions and a clean light theme for daytime use. Pick what suits you in Settings, or follow your device.

  • System — matches your phone or laptop automatically
  • Light — bright backgrounds for well-lit environments
  • Dark — easy on the eyes at night or on OLED screens

How it works

From voice to fluency

Follow Hungarian conversations with live English text, then practice with courses and quizzes — all in an installable web app you control.

Live translation

Capture Hungarian conversation through Soniox STT and read English in real time on the Translate screen — with follow-along mode for longer discussions.

Courses & quiz

Work through alphabet, numbers, and phrases, then drill vocabulary with spaced practice.

Your deploy

Ship as a Cloudflare Worker with static assets and a small Hono API. Secrets stay on your account.

Get started

Deploy your own

Clone the repo, add your Soniox key and VAPID keys, create a D1 database, then npm run deploy. Full steps live in the README.
